A key pillar of Jal Jeevan Mission is community-led water 
management. Across rural India, trained Nal Jal Mitras are 
playing a vital role in the operation, maintenance, and repair

reliable tap water services at the grassroots. 
By building local technical capacity and strengthening commu-
nity ownership, Nal Jal Mitras are helping secure the long-
term success of the Mission while empowering villages to 
manage their own water resources effectively.
