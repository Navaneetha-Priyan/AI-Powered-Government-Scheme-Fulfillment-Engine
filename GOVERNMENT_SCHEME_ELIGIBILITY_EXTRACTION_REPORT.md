## SCHEME 2: PM-Vishwakarma (Artisans Scheme)

### Scheme Details
- **Scheme ID**: PM-Vishwakarma
- **Official Name**: PM Vishwakarma Yojana
- **Description**: Artisans and Craftspeople Scheme
- **PDF File**: PM_Vishwakarma_Yojana.pdf
- **Total Pages**: 64
- **Extraction Status**: ✅ **COMPLETE** - Comprehensive scheme guidelines document

### Beneficiary Scope
**PRIMARY**: ARTISAN / CRAFTSPERSON

**Definition**: "An artisan or craftsperson working with hands and tools and engaged in one of the family-based traditional trades specified in the scheme, in the unorganized or informal sector, on self-employment basis."

**Covered Trades** (18 traditional trades):
1. Carpenter (Suthar/Badhai) - Wood based
2. Blacksmith (Lohar) - Iron/Metal/Stone based
3. Boat Maker
4. Armourer
5. Hammer and Tool Kit Maker
6. Locksmith
7. Goldsmith (Sonar)
8. Potter (Kumhaar)
9. Sculptor/Stone Carver/Stone Breaker (Moortikar)
10. Cobbler/Shoesmith/Footwear Artisan (Charmakar)
11. Mason (Rajmistri)
12. Basket/Mat/Broom Maker/Coir Weaver
13. Doll & Toy Maker (Traditional)
14. Barber (Naai)
15. Garland Maker (Malakaar)
16. Washerman (Dhobi)
17. Tailor (Darzi)
18. Fishing Net Maker

### Primary Eligibility Criteria

**REQUIRED Criteria:**

1. **Age Requirement**
   - Minimum age: **18 years** on the date of registration
   - Maximum age: NOT SPECIFIED

2. **Engagement in Trade**
   - Must be engaged in specified trade on the date of registration
   - Must be actively working in the unorganized/informal sector
   - Must be on self-employment basis

3. **No Prior Credit-Based Scheme Availed**
   - Should NOT have availed loans under similar credit-based central/state government schemes for self-employment/business development
   - Examples: PMEGP, MUDRA, SVANidhi
   - **Exception**: Beneficiaries of MUDRA and SVANidhi who have fully repaid their loan are eligible (5-year waiting period from loan sanction date applies)

4. **Family Restriction**
   - Registration and benefits restricted to ONE member per family
   - **Family Definition**: Husband, wife, and unmarried children
   - This is a key criterion - only one eligible person per family can be registered

5. **Citizenship**
   - Must be Indian Citizen
   - **Note**: Indian nationality requirement clearly specified

6. **Employment Status**
   - Person in government service and family members shall NOT be eligible

7. **Formal Registration**
   - Must undergo registration under PM Vishwakarma Portal
   - Must provide required documents for enrolment

### Exclusion Criteria

**Explicitly NOT Eligible:**
1. **Government Employees**: A person in government service and their family members shall NOT be eligible under the scheme

**Conditionally Excluded:**
2. Persons who have already availed similar credit schemes (with exception for MUDRA/SVANidhi beneficiaries who have repaid)

### Age Restrictions
- **Minimum Age**: **18 years**
- **Maximum Age**: No maximum specified

### Gender Restrictions
- **General Eligibility**: Scheme open to both male and female artisans
- **Special Promotion**: Scheme actively promotes empowerment of women and marginalized groups including SC, ST, OBC, specially abled, transgenders, residents of NER states, island territories, and hilly areas

### Income Limits
**NOT SPECIFIED** - No explicit income ceiling mentioned in eligibility criteria. Focus is on informal/unorganized sector workers.

### Social Category Preferences
- **Yes - Proactive Inclusion**: Scheme seeks to promote empowerment of:
  - Scheduled Castes (SC)
  - Scheduled Tribes (ST)
  - Other Backward Classes (OBC)
  - Specially Abled persons
  - Transgenders
  - Residents of North Eastern (NER) states
  - Island Territories
  - Hilly Areas

### Geographic Scope
- **Rural/Urban**: BOTH rural and urban areas
- **Implementation Strategy**: Phased district-wise saturation approach
- **State Specific**: All India scheme (ALL STATES)
- **Special Focus**: NER states, island territories, and hilly areas given priority

### Citizenship Requirement

### Required Documents

**For Enrolment**:
1. Aadhaar card/number
2. Mobile number
3. Bank account details (Aadhaar-seeded bank account)
4. Ration card
   - If ration card not available: Alternative address proof
5. Details of all family members

**Additional Documents**:

### Education Requirements
- **Formal Education**: NO SPECIFIC REQUIREMENT
- **Skill-Based**: Existing skill and hands-on experience in the trade required
- **Training Requirement**: Basic Training of 5-7 days is MANDATORY for:
  - Eligibility condition for drawing first loan tranche
  - Skill upgradation and modernization

- **Mentioned in Criteria**: YES - Family definition includes "husband, wife and unmarried children"
- **Specific Restriction**: Only married/family head considerations, but no explicit marital status bar for eligibility

### Additional Requirements & Benefits

- Basic Training (5-7 days, 40 hours)
- Board and lodging provided FREE
- Wage compensation: Rs. 500 stipend/day

**Benefits Structure:**
1. **Recognition**: PM Vishwakarma Certificate and ID Card
2. **Skill Upgradation**: Training and certification
3. **Toolkit Incentive**: Up to Rs. 15,000 (after skill assessment)
4. **Credit Support**:

- **Launch Date**: September 1, 2024 (PMAY-U 2.0)

### Beneficiary Scope
**PRIMARY BENEFICIARIES**: Urban poor and middle-class families in urban areas

**Income Categories Eligible**:
1. **EWS** (Economically Weaker Section)
2. **LIG** (Low Income Group)
3. **MIG** (Middle Income Group)

**Beneficiary Definition**: Families belonging to EWS, LIG, and MIG categories requiring urban housing assistance

### Primary Eligibility Criteria

**REQUIRED Criteria:**

1. **Residential Status**
   - Beneficiary must be residing in urban area
   - Urban Local Body (ULB)/Municipality jurisdiction requirement

2. **Income Category Classification**
   - Must fall within EWS, LIG, or MIG income categories
   - Income limits to be defined by State/UT
   - Based on Economically Weaker Section classification

3. **Housing Status**
   - Aimed at urban poor lacking adequate housing

4. **Beneficiary Identification**
   - Through transparent process
   - Verification by competent authority (ULB)
   - As per eligibility criteria defined in scheme

5. **Project Eligibility**
   - Must apply for whitelisted/approved projects
   - Whitelisted projects meet all scheme eligibility criteria

### Exclusion Criteria

**Explicitly NOT Eligible:**

1. **Prior Housing Benefit Recipients**: 
   - Persons who have already availed benefits under this AHP (Affordable Housing in Partnership) project of PMAY-U are NOT eligible

2. **Multiple Project Applicants**: 
   - Cannot apply for same project multiple times

3. **Income Above Ceiling**: 
   - Persons with family income exceeding defined EWS/LIG/MIG categories

### Age Restrictions
- **Minimum Age**: NOT EXPLICITLY SPECIFIED
- **Maximum Age**: NOT EXPLICITLY SPECIFIED
- **Presumed**: Adult family heads apply; family-based eligibility

### Gender Restrictions
- **No Gender Bar**: Scheme open to all genders
- **Emphasis**: Women-headed households may receive priority consideration

### Income Limits
- **Defined by**: State/UT Governments
- **Categories**:
  - **EWS** (Economically Weaker Section) - Lowest income tier
  - **LIG** (Low Income Group) - Middle-low income tier
  - **MIG** (Middle Income Group) - Middle income tier
- **Exact Amounts**: NOT SPECIFIED IN PDF (varies by state)

### Social Category Preferences
- **General Scheme**: Not explicitly categorized by caste/social group in extracted content
- **Emphasis**: Focus on economically weaker sections (income-based, not caste-based)

### Geographic Scope
- **Coverage**: URBAN areas only
- **Geographic Level**: 
  - Urban Local Bodies (ULBs)/Municipalities
  - Across the country
  - All States/UTs eligible to participate
- **Rural/Urban**: EXCLUSIVELY URBAN

### Citizenship Requirement
- **Required**: YES - Indian Citizen (implied in "eligible beneficiaries")

### Required Documents
**For Application:**
1. Proof of residence in urban area
2. Income proof/certificate (for EWS/LIG/MIG classification)
3. Identity proof (Aadhaar/Voter ID/PAN)
4. Family composition details
5. Bank account details
6. Any other documents as prescribed by ULB

**Project Types:**

1. **Affordable Housing in Partnership (AHP) Model-1**:
   - Construction by public sector agencies and parastatals
   - Allocation to eligible EWS beneficiaries

2. **Affordable Housing in Partnership (AHP) Model-2**:
   - Private developers undertake projects on PPP mode
   - On own encumbrance-free land
   - Exclusive EWS houses in whitelisted projects

**House Specifications**:
- **Type**: All-weather pucca houses
- **Carpet Area**: 30-45 sqm
- **Basic Amenities**: Included with basic civic services and social infrastructure
- **Materials**: Roof and walls made of sturdy, modern, durable materials (burnt bricks, cement concrete, GI sheets, etc.)

**Beneficiary Support**:
- **Allocation Method**: Transparent process by State/UT
- **Selection**: Based on eligibility criteria and lottery/draw if needed
- **Verification**: By ULBs and competent authority

**Financial Assistance**:
- **Redeemable Housing Vouchers (RHV)**: Provided to EWS beneficiaries
- **Central Assistance**: Government-provided through vouchers
- **State Contribution**: State/UT governments contribute their share
- **Innovation Grant**: Technology Innovation Grant (TIG) of Rs. 1,000/sqm (up to 30 sqm per dwelling unit) for innovative construction

**Implementation Timeline**:
- **Launch**: September 1, 2024
- **Target**: Assist additional 1 crore (10 million) urban households
